- **DEX and app-perf reads no longer present a failed Postgres read as healthy or empty data.**
  - **Device experience score:** on a degraded read, the device-page DEX lens now shows "DEX store degraded.", `GET /api/v1/dex/devices/{id}` answers `503` (`retry_after_ms: 2000`) and MCP `get_dex_device_score` returns a retryable error. Previously all three showed a fabricated healthy score of 100 with no signals (#4855). The score and its signal list now come from one read, so they can no longer disagree.
  - **`/dex` overview and health:** devices whose score could not be computed are counted and shown ("N device(s) could not be scored"), and `unscored` is added to the overview JSON. Before, they silently dropped out of the fleet figures.
  - **App-perf group trend and `/auto` VERIFY compare:** a failed management-group member lookup is now reported as degraded instead of as "no member reported" or 0 members (#1762).
  - **`/dex/perf/app` device-model picker:** it now lists the models from the same data as `GET /api/v1/dex/perf/cohorts`, rather than a fleet-wide tag query outside the API layer (#4857). When no devices are reporting, it says "no reporting devices this cycle" instead of silently hiding. A model with only historical data can still be opened by URL.
  - **Metrics:** `yuzu_server_mgmt_group_read_degrade_total` now also counts member-read degrades.
