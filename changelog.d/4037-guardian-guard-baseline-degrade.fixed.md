- **Guardian per-guard and per-Baseline detail pages now distinguish a degraded status read from "no devices report this guard/Baseline" (#4037 governance finding SEC-2).** `/guardian/guard/{id}` and `/guardian/baseline/{id}` previously collapsed a failed `agent_rule_statuses` read to an empty census, rendering identically to a guard or Baseline that genuinely has zero reporting agents — an operator could not tell "nothing to report" from "the read failed." Both pages now render a distinct degraded placeholder instead, matching the posture the fleet-wide guards-overview page and the per-device Guardian lens already had.
