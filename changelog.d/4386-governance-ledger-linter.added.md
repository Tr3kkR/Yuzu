- **`scripts/ci/check-governance-ledger.py`** — an author-side linter for a `governance.d/` run-ledger fragment that previews the self-consistency problems a strict reviewer flags, so a ledger converges in fewer review rounds. It reads a finding's live view as the field-wise merge SKILL.md mandates (ordered by `recorded_at`; list fields replace wholesale; the five attestation fields row-scoped and exempt) and checks: `severity_mapped` not weaker than the facts' derived floor band and empty-impact/floor consistency; `severity_native` frozen across supersessions (per row); a `wording` finding never carrying `I7`; the Gate-7 park constraint (an `I1`/`I2`/`I3` finding may not be `roadmap-`parked); single-value `reporter`; per-row `adjudicated_by`↔`adjudication_rationale` pairing; and `source`/enum/`policy_floor`-key hygiene, skipping pre-`schema_version` legacy rows. It is deliberately ADVISORY, not a blocking corpus gate — the `/governance` skill runs it on the current run's fragment as a Gate-8 pre-push step (`--files "$LEDGER"`, fail-closed), and only its self-test runs in CI. Diff-scoped discovery fails closed on a bad base ref rather than silently reporting an empty scope.
