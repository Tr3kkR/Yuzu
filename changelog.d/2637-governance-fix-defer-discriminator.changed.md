- `/governance` Gate 7 now carries a fix-now-vs-defer discriminator: a finding is judged on whether it is a *correction* to code already under review or adds a *mechanism* (a probe, gate, fixture, dependency or new call path), and on whether the change would still be worth merging without it. Corrections fold; mechanism splits; mechanism that IS the deliverable means the scope was drawn wrong and the change should be re-cut. The post-run section's "typically produces 8-15 deferred follow-up items" expectation is removed — it functioned as a quota that made filing the default disposition
