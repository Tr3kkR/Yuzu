The overlay narrows rather than closes the pre-inert window, so the reader rule in the R5.7 consumer preconditions still applies. R5.7 also records the measured behaviour of the File watch on rename, move, remove and SMB (a rename or move leaves coverage at `Notification` while the target path is unwatched; resolution tracked in #4676). New Windows-only chaos tests CH-1, CH-4, CH-6, CH-8 and CH-11 characterise the establishment signal (#4659).
