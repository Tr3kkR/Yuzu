- The dashboard exec console (`POST /api/dashboard/execute`) now enforces the same Destructive-class targeting rule as `/api/command` and the MCP `execute_instruction` tool: a `plugin.action` classified `Destructive` must name an explicit, in-scope agent, and a fleet broadcast (`__all__`), a management-group scope, or an omitted target is refused rather than dispatched. Previously the console was the one operator-facing dispatch surface without that rule, so any holder of a destructive action's declared securable could fan `tar.purge_source`, `registry.delete_key`, `filesystem.delete_lines`, `tags.clear`, `storage.clear` and similar actions across the whole fleet from a single command line. Targets are additionally confined to the operator's visible agents, failing closed when that read is unavailable or degraded. Refusals are counted on `yuzu_server_dispatch_target_rejected_total{route="dashboard"}` and written to the audit log. `ReadOnly` and `Mutating` actions are unchanged, and scheduled or background dispatch of destructive actions by scope is deliberately unaffected.
