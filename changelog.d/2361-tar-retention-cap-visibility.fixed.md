- TAR retention now reports when its per-pass cap binds. Capping deletion at 5,000 rows per table per pass bounds how long the rollup holds the database mutex, but it also means a table gaining more than that per 900 s tick grows without bound - and neither the declines nor the failures counter moves in that state, so the endpoint reported perfectly healthy while `tar.db` grew. `tar status` now carries `retention_guard_capped_total` plus per-table `retention_guard_capped|<table>|<n>` lines, and the rollup warns when the cap binds. Deliberately kept out of the failures total: "not being retained" and "retained too slowly" call for different operator actions.
