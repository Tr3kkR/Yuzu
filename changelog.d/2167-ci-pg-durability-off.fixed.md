- Fixed the Windows/MSVC CI leg failing intermittently (≈39% of recent runs, Windows-only) when the `[pg]` server-test shard was killed at its meson timeout. The shard budget had been cut to 600s on the assumption that per-agent Postgres removed cross-job contention, but that provisioning was not yet live on the Windows runner, and at full durability every `[pg]` case's `CREATE DATABASE … TEMPLATE` + `DROP DATABASE WITH (FORCE)` is fsync-bound (~20× costlier on Windows). The disposable CI Postgres clusters now run with `fsync`/`synchronous_commit`/`full_page_writes` off — the docker (Linux) and brew (macOS) clusters in `scripts/ci/ensure-postgres.sh`, and the native Windows service (agent-0 + per-agent clusters) in `deploy/windows/Provision-Windows-Runner.ps1`. The per-test pristine-database isolation is unchanged — only its durability cost is removed. The `[pg]` shard budget is restored to 900s as a transitional net until per-agent Postgres is provisioned on the Windows box. The pre-set-DSN escape hatch (`YUZU_TEST_POSTGRES_DSN`, for a runner with a bespoke native Postgres install) now hard-fails the job if the DSN sets `options=` or `PGOPTIONS` is set in the job environment - either silently disables PgPool's `statement_timeout`/`lock_timeout` safety bounds instead of just degrading durability.
