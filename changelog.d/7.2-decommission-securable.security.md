- **`DELETE /api/v1/sle/agents/{id}`'s agent-decommission gate is now the single, dedicated `Decommission:Delete` securable, replacing a hand-maintained three-way conjunction that stopped scaling (PLAN-01 ruling (b)).** The route's gate had been `SoftwareLicensing:Delete` AND `Inventory:Delete` AND `GuaranteedState:Delete` — one Delete grant per securable the cascade erases through, kept in lockstep with the store list by a drift guard (ADR-0024 Decision 9). That decision recorded its own reversal trigger: "revisit if the conjunction ever reaches a fourth securable." It did — `AppUsageStore` (the app-usage last-used projection, Wave 7 PR7.2) is governed by the new `Forensics` securable, and the cascade now erases six per-agent stores (inventory, software_inventory, device_inventory, app_perf_daily, software_licensing, app_usage) rather than five. The gate is now one scoped check, `Decommission:Delete`, authorizing for the cascade's whole blast radius regardless of how many stores it grows to cover; a 403 now names `Decommission:Delete` directly rather than one of three possible conjuncts.
  **Compat story.** Seeded roles are unaffected: Administrator and ITServiceOwner could decommission before this change and still can — `seed_defaults()` runs on every `RbacStore` construction (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, with `revoked_seed_defaults` suppression respected), so the upgrade grants both roles `Decommission:Delete` with no migration required. Operator-authored custom roles are **breaking by design**: a role that had assembled the three old per-store Delete grants specifically to reach this route is now refused (403 naming `Decommission:Delete`) until granted the new securable — the promotion's entire point is one nameable grant for what the operation destroys, not an implicit bundle of grants an operator had to reconstruct by reading the cascade's source. The three old grants are **not revoked** and continue gating their own stores' other routes exactly as before.
