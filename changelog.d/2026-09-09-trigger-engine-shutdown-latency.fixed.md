- **Agent shuts down promptly instead of stalling up to 5 seconds.** `TriggerEngine`'s worker loops (interval, file-watch, service-watch, registry-watch) polled on a fixed `sleep_for()` and only noticed a stop request on their next wake — on non-Windows the registry-watch no-op loop alone could hold up shutdown by a full 5 seconds. All four loops now block on a condition variable that `stop()` wakes immediately.
