- **Wi-Fi scanning now reads NetworkManager directly over D-Bus on Linux, falling back automatically to nmcli/iw when unavailable, and no longer spawns a shell anywhere in the plugin.** macOS scan keeps its airport/system_profiler sources but invokes them as direct argv through the bounded subprocess runner. A Wi-Fi query that cannot be answered — NetworkManager unreachable and every command-line fallback failing — now reports an explicit "could not be determined" record instead of a confident "Not connected", and WPA3 (SAE) and enhanced-open (OWE) networks are now reported distinctly rather than as WPA2.
