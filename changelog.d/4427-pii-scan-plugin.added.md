- **`pii_scan` plugin: sensitive-data (PII) discovery.** Audits files against a research-backed ruleset (credit cards, IBAN, ICAO passport MRZ, driver's licences, and national identifiers for 60 countries) using RE2 shape matching plus real checksum/structural validation where one exists — a shape match that fails validation is dropped, not just downgraded. Findings never carry the raw matched value, only a masked form. Optional realtime scanning re-invokes a scan when new files appear under a watched directory, built on the agent's existing filesystem-trigger primitive.
