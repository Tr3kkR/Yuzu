### 🔴 Blocker

**1. `detach_rule_locked` durably removes the rule before it owns the disarm (Codex C1, Kimi confirmed-independently, synthesizer verified).** `guardian_spark_runtime.cpp:1160-1162` runs `index_->remove_rule` and `rules_.erase`, then `:1181-1190` allocates the Disarm claim (`make_shared<KeyClaim>`, `claims_[*disarm_key]`, `fifo.push_back`) with no try/catch, and `keys_.erase` follows at `:1195`. A throw in `:1181-1190` strands the live subscription in `keys_` with no rule, no index entry and no claim: nothing will ever disarm it, and the next same-key attach takes the 0->1 claim path whose completion hits the new `keys_.emplace` hard error (a spurious `logic_error` to the caller; the OLD subscription is the one leaked, the new one is reclaimed). Anchor: design §R5.2 ("a disarm claim is retained until it actually executes"); `docs/cpp-conventions.md` ownership; the #3831 rollback-before-mutation contract; policy floor (ownership/lifetime defect of the kind `cpp-safety` blocks). Fix: build the claim and `claims_.try_emplace(key)` BEFORE `remove_rule`/`rules_.erase` (all fallible work first), then perform the nothrow mutations; any remaining fallible step after the durable removal gets a catch that disarms the subscription directly and counts it (the existing last-resort shape), so an unwind can never orphan `keys_[key]`. Falsifier: an allocation-fault seam at `:1181` with a real subscription; assert `disarms == 1`, `keys_` empty, and a fresh same-key attach arming cleanly.

**2. `release_claim_index_locked` clears `index_held` before the fallible removal (Codex C2, Kimi confirmed-independently, synthesizer verified).** `:109` sets `claim.index_held = false`, then `:110` calls `index_->remove_rule`, which copies the key into a `std::string` before mutating (`spark_key_rule_index.hpp:142`, documented as its one throwing step). A throw leaves the mapping present with the flag cleared, so every later cleanup (the drain's publish retry, `begin_stop`, detach) no-ops on it; a different rule attaching to that key then sees a non-edge with no `keys_` entry and throws from `keys_.at` (the 7.7b item-3 ghost-entry class). Fix: call `remove_rule` first and clear the flag only after it returns (registry serialization makes the two-statement window safe). Falsifier: fault the key copy on a refused arm; assert the next same-key attach arms.
